# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

Examples of positive behavior:
- Constructive technical feedback
- Respectful communication
- Collaborative problem solving

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Personal attacks or abusive language
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code of conduct.
